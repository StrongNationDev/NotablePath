About NotablePath

Independent research for clearer Wikipedia decisions.

NotablePath helps people and organizations understand the evidence, editorial standards, and practical choices involved in Wikipedia-related work.

We are an independent consultancy. Wikipedia and the Wikimedia Foundation are not affiliated with or endorsing our services.

Who we are

A focused, founder-led consultancy.

NotablePath is an independent, founder-led Wikipedia research, editing, and consultation service. Clients work directly with the consultancy. When a project calls for additional research, writing, technical, or reputation expertise, trusted specialists may contribute to that specific brief.

We do not present ourselves as a large agency, and we distinguish our professional services from Wikipedia's volunteer-led editorial process.

Our mission

Make the evidence and available options easier to understand.

Wikipedia decisions depend on published sources, notability standards, editorial policies, and independent judgment. Our purpose is to help clients understand how those factors may apply to their situation, identify what the evidence can support, and choose a realistic next step.

Our approach

A considered process, from evidence to next steps.

  1. 01

    Research the Evidence

    Gather relevant published information and understand the question the project needs to answer.

  2. 02

    Assess the Sources

    Review source independence, reliability, depth, and relevance to the subject.

  3. 03

    Provide Clear Recommendations

    Explain findings, limitations, and practical options in neutral, plain language.

  4. 04

    Support the Next Step

    Where appropriate, provide assessment, editorial guidance, or draft-development support.

Our principles

Careful work, clear boundaries.

  • Research-led recommendations. Advice begins with evidence, not assumptions about the outcome.
  • Neutral and verifiable writing. Editorial support prioritizes clear language and reliable published sources.
  • Transparency. We explain limitations and when a particular route may not be appropriate.
  • Respect for editorial independence. Wikipedia editors make their own decisions; we cannot guarantee publication, approval, or retention.
