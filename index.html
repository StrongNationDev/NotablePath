A clear sequence

How the Process Works

A straightforward process designed to help you understand your position and make informed decisions.

  1. 01

    Tell Us What You Need

    Explain your Wikipedia-related problem through our guided consultation. We review your initial information and clarify the scope of your project.

  2. 02

    We Research and Assess

    We examine relevant published sources, the supplied article or draft where available, and applicable Wikipedia policies and guidelines.

  3. 03

    Receive Your Findings and Recommendations

    Your report explains the current position, what needs improvement, what the evidence supports, and recommended next steps.

  4. 04Optional

    Optional Editing and Implementation

    If requested and appropriate, we carry out agreed editorial improvements and explain how revised material can proceed through Wikipedia's editorial process.
