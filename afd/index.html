An article nominated for deletion needs to be evaluated against the evidence and the reason for nomination. The useful question is not simply whether the subject is well known. The useful question is whether the available independent, reliable evidence supports an encyclopedic treatment under Wikipedia's standards.

The deletion discussion is already underway. Reviewing the evidence sooner gives you a clearer understanding of the situation and the options available.
